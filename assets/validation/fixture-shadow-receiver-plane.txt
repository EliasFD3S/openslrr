Issue #201 / PR #202: fixture shadow receiver-plane correction (2026-11-19)

Cause/evidence:
- User reported horizontal stripes on the car after stronger neon lighting.
- Isolated game captures with identical camera, active reflections and only
  fixture shadows toggled reproduce/remove the bands. Before: stripe-before.png;
  fixture shadows disabled: stripe-no-shadow.png (both under build/).
- Existing fixture PCF compared neighbouring depths against a single reference.
  Wide 160-degree perspective/512-square tiles project large, sloping receiver
  footprints on near-vertical body panels, producing self-shadow bands.

Change:
- Derive projected receiver-plane depth gradients before varying bounds branches.
- Four texel-centred comparisons each use that texel's plane depth, then bilinear
  weights interpolate their results. Hidden hardware PCF neighbours no longer
  need the same incorrect reference. A small .000005 residual depth bias remains.
- Existing normal/radial offsets, intensity, range, atlas size and four-tap budget
  retained. Vehicle casting/reception, real blockers and cubemap capture stay on.

Validation:
- build/stripe-baseline-gpu.log: new grazing vehicle-panel regression fails on
  the previous implementation: 9724 affected pixels out of 16384.
- build/stripe-fix-gpu.log: exit 0; panel stripes=0; actual garage blocked pixels
  4986. Nine individual sources, cache/lifetime, white emission, sun and flare
  tests also pass.
- build/stripe-corrected.png: same isolated game/camera inspected, body bands
  removed while actual furniture/vehicle shadows remain. Diagnostic processes
  deliberately stopped; not a clean-exit or matched performance result.
- Temporary camera/capture/shadow-toggle instrumentation removed.
- Final production build, tests, ownership, knowledge and smoke run recorded
  separately in build/stripe-final-*.log.

Limits:
- This targets fixture shadow acne, not every reflection/material artifact.
- The car's original mesh normals/materials and cubemap resolution are unchanged.
- No matched frame-time improvement is claimed; user acceptance remains pending.
