Two runs of the same backend and settings (clouds and the car's reflection move): Direct3D 11 0.113 (16.0 %),
Vulkan 0.009 (0.2 %).

Reading: about 1.4 of the 1.9 comes from anisotropic filtering, about 0.25 from AO, the rest from effects
that move between runs. With all five off the two backends give the same image. Both backends ask for the same
maximum anisotropy (bgfx: 16 on Direct3D 11, the device limit, 16, on Vulkan; no LOD bias on either).

Side observation, not a backend difference: MSAA 8x against MSAA off changes 0.2 % of the pixels of this
capture on Direct3D 11 and 0.3 % on Vulkan (mean 0.03). Not investigated.
