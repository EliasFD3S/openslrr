Where the Vulkan ambient_occlusion difference appears (#186)

Method: scene_shadow_gpu run up to and including ambient_occlusion, with the AO targets and the three
captures of each wall camera read back (temporary test code, not committed). 80x60 AO targets, 160x120 view.
"differ" = texels or pixels whose value is not identical; "max" = largest difference out of 255.

Direct3D 11 against Direct3D 12 (RTX 4060): 0 differ at every stage, for every camera.

Vulkan against Direct3D 12 (RTX 4060):
stage                                         frontal near HBAO   frontal near GTAO   frontal far on floor HBAO
linear depth, R32F (AO geometry pass)         0 of 4800           0 of 4800           0 of 4800
normal, RGBA32F (AO geometry pass)            0 of 4800           0 of 4800           0 of 4800
capture without AO                            0 px                0 px                0 px
AO after the first filter (ao_filter_tex_)    68, max 11          80, max 8           45, max 127
AO after the second filter (ao_raw_tex_)      165, max 8          200, max 6          211, max 51
capture, reference sampling                   523 px, max 4       800 px, max 6       656 px, max 58
capture, AO-resolution sampling               744 px, max 8       642 px, max 6       652 px, max 47
(the other wall cameras without a floor: normals differ by one unit in the last place in at most 663 texels,
captures identical)

Where: after the first (horizontal) filter the difference is one texel row: row 0, the top border, for the
frontal near camera, and row 33, the last wall row above the floor edge (linear depth 39.95 m above, 26.99 m
below), for the frontal far camera. The second (vertical) filter spreads it to rows 0-2 and 31-35. On that row
Direct3D gives 255 (no occlusion), Vulkan 232 to 245.

Same adapter question: on the Intel Iris Xe (8086:a7a0) the Vulkan AO differs from Direct3D 11 in the same
texels (164 / 200 / 211, max 8 / 6 / 51), and the Vulkan result is the same on both GPUs (0 to 6 texels differ,
max 1). It is therefore not one driver.

shaderc -O 0 and -O 3 give a SPIR-V fs_ao of the same size (167,823 bytes of header) and the same AO result.

Conclusion: with identical depth, normals and uniforms supplied by the host, the first AO pass of
shaders/fs_ao.sc (pass 0, hbao()/gtao() and sampleDelta()) computes a different value in SPIR-V than in DXBC
and DXIL, on one texel row next to a vertical depth step or the top border. The cause inside that pass is not
found.

Test isolation: ambient_occlusion run alone (other cases skipped) fails on Direct3D 11 and Direct3D 12 as well
("shaded>10 && shaded<width*height/2", GTAO contact pixels 0), with other AO counts than in the full run. The
case depends on state left by the cases before it.
