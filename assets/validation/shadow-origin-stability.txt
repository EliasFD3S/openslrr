#201 stationary-camera moving-sun edge shimmer follow-up
Windows x64 Release / D3D11; previous PR #202 head 5a67025.
User confirms: camera/car stationary, shimmer mainly at edges; stopping clock
stops the shimmer and improves performance. Earlier wider PCF alone was insufficient.

A numerical stationary receiver at (5000,20,-7000), 400 tiny sun-direction
changes, reproduces a maximum lattice jump of 0.89844 tile texels with the
old global origin. The same sequence around the local origin: 0.00372 texels.
A long-drive aligned rebase preserves all four cascade phases within 0.00073
texels in the fixture. These are projection tests, not a full visual camera test.

Changes: shared nearby floating origin for sun-caster and receiver coordinates;
depth vertex shader separates model/world and view-projection multiplication;
absolute-space culling retained. Rebase travel on the common tile-grid step.
Use car depth comparison before bilinear filtering instead of interpolated raw
depth followed by step(). GPU sub-texel car edge: 7 distinct coverage values.

Atlas reuse requires exact matrices, origin and ordered caster snapshots
(source/revision, buffer handles and transforms). No time/angular threshold.
A stable redraw establishes the snapshot after a moving-light interval.
Real Test Track integration confirms unchanged reuse and invalidation by a small
sun change, a moved shell and a geometry revision. No frozen moving geometry.
GPU tests also cover nonzero-origin reception, cascades, blending/fade, previews,
emission/local light and thin stripes. Existing shader ownership/reopen passes
with 28 baseline shaders (one additional depth vertex shader).
