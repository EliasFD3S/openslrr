Scene environment / exterior eligibility (#201, PR #202)

Problem: exterior lighting required registered stock ground props, and the
shadow light was taken from a visible ground_polygons instance. SCX-only addon
scenes and dealer scenes could therefore miss the exterior path.

Change: mainloop forwards Scene.java internalScene when present; preview scenes
opt out. Without metadata, retain automatic stock-ground detection. The shadow
pass uses the active root-scoped scene light directly. No Java patch.
Garage local-light approximation and real emitter work are unchanged.
