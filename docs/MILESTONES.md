# Milestones (draft for refinement)

Drafted 12 November 2026 from `main` at `b907b6b`, the native audit
(329 bodies, 4 no-ops, 43 missing of 376), open issues #15–#19 and the
roadmap in [NATIVE_AUDIT.md](NATIVE_AUDIT.md#roadmap). Evidence for each
item is in [KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md](KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md) and
[EXTERNAL_RESEARCH.md](EXTERNAL_RESEARCH.md). This is a proposal: order,
scope and exit criteria are open to the maintainers.

Every item starts by checking whether SLRR_rev already implements it and
migrating it when it does ([EXTERNAL_RESEARCH.md §7](EXTERNAL_RESEARCH.md#7-migrating-mechanics-from-slrr_rev)
maps the current gaps). Every milestone ends with the same two checks,
reported separately: the Release x64 build, and an in-game validation of the
named scenario.

## M0 — Reproducible build and verification baseline

Goal: a fresh clone builds, and every claim about progress can be
regenerated.

- Track `tools/compile_game_sources.cpp` or make its CMake target optional;
  today a clean clone cannot configure (`CMakeLists.txt`, `.gitignore`).
- Commit the native-coverage generator the README names, with a pinned stock
  native table, so the 329/4/43 figures can be checked. PR #3 has a design
  for this.
- Fix the malformed last `.gitignore` line, and correct the stale statements
  listed in [KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md §9](KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md#9-where-the-documentation-disagrees-with-the-code).
- Decide whether `main` gets a test target and CI again. `main` had a CTest
  suite until `b907b6b` deleted it, and CI only ever existed on the PR #3
  branch; the probes are all that remain.
- Keep `scripts/check_knowledge_refs.py` passing.

Done when: a clean clone configures and builds `slrr_host` with the README
steps, the coverage figures are regenerated by a tracked tool, and the
knowledge-base check passes.

Question for refinement: should tests and CI return, and in what form?

## M1 — Playable Test Track drive

Goal: one stock car drives the Test Track with validated input, HUD and
plausible acceleration.

- #15 HUD size and alignment: PR #20 is open.
- Steering, brake, clutch, shifting and the input profile validated through
  a relaunch, on keyboard and a physical pad (NATIVE_AUDIT roadmap step 1).
- Nitro activation and consumption connected to driving.
- Compare acceleration, braking and shifts with the original-game telemetry
  corpus (Baiern, Nonus, Einvagen, Duhen) at two or more frame rates.
- Settle the dyno disagreement (final table endpoint, nitro argument)
  against the MWM binary, not against SLRR_rev.
- Wall collision, impact damage and mileage currently run only for the
  player-controlled car with an engine
  (`src/resources/part_runtime.cpp:advance_chassis_tick`); uncontrolled or
  engineless cars never hit walls. This also matters for M2 and M4.

Done when: the Baiern drives a lap on keyboard and pad, its 0–100 km/h time
is within an agreed tolerance of the original game's at 60 and 144 FPS, and
the HUD matches the reviewed screenshots. The published 6.1 s figure comes
from Steam build 940; an MWM capture should confirm it first.

## M2 — World collision and repair

Goal: the car collides with placed world objects, and garage repair restores
a damaged car.

- #16: keep CFG `body` shapes (box, sphere, rod, mesh, `nocollision`) for
  map placements and build a static collider index independent of render
  culling and LOD. Use a volume test so 0.1 m tree trunks cannot pass
  between probes. A plan is posted on the issue and awaits approval.
- Bind the seven missing `PhysicsRef` natives and make
  `GameRef.setActiveCollision` real, as the maintainer asked on #16.
- Extend `GameRef.setState` beyond cars to generic dynamic objects.
- #17: add the native `repair` part command that stock `Part.repair()`
  sends, restoring deformation, slot damage, wheel damage and detached
  panels for the repaired subtree. A plan is posted on the issue.
- Connect impact wear to parts and the regional HUD damage display.

Done when: in Valo City the car stops against trees, lamp posts and
containers at low and high speed; a crashed car leaves the garage repair
visually restored; FPS does not regress.

## M3 — City performance

Goal: driving through Valo City stays well above 60 FPS with VSync off,
with buildings and nearby trees drawn and the far plane at 3 km (#18).

- Measure first: CPU time per phase (scripts, physics, scene collection,
  submission) before and after each change.
- Build the scene instance list once per frame and reuse it for the main
  view, reflections and shadows; cache world matrices instead of
  recomputing every parent chain with an allocation.
- Instance alpha-cutout trees, pool identical materials and share one GPU
  texture per DDS (SLRR_rev measured large reductions from the last two).
- Remove per-frame linear scans (`gameref_find_live`, native alias lookup)
  where the profile shows them.

Done when: the #18 route meets the issue's criteria, with Before and After
metrics and no visual regression.

## M4 — Navigation, traffic and pedestrians

Goal: routes, traffic and pedestrians come from the map data and the stock
natives.

- Load the city road network with the existing reader
  (`src/formats/trc.cpp:trc_read`) and implement `getNearestCross` with the
  documented contract.
- Implement the `GroundRef` route queries (`findRoute`, `getRouteLength`,
  `getRoutePos`, `getRouteDist`, `alignToRoad`, `getStartDirection`),
  `Navigator.updateNavigator` and `RenderRef.plotRoute`, then verify the
  map, rear view and cruise control in a race.
- #19: keep pedestrians on the sidewalks, add `pedestrianDistance` and
  `removePedestrianType`, and let placement and walking come from the stock
  natives and Animation.
- Traffic natives (`addTrafficN/P`, `addTrafficCar`, `delTraffic`,
  `haltTrafficCross` and the rest) after collision (M2) and performance
  (M3) are stable. Traffic lights read crossing phases.
- Water (`setWater`, `addWaterLimit`).

Done when: a city race shows a correct route and GPS, traffic follows lanes
and lights, and pedestrians stay off the roadway.

## M5 — Garage and career loop

Goal: a career can buy, tune, paint, repair, race, save and reload a car.

- `Painter` natives (`doPaint`, `paintPart`, `xPaint`) and decal
  persistence through saves.
- `ParticleSystem` natives (nine signatures), flares, `GroundRef.setFog`
  read by the renderer, and part loop sounds (`Part.setSfxLoopParams`).
- End-to-end validation: purchase, installed parts, paint and condition
  survive a career save and reload; the preview car never becomes the
  career car.
- Save durability: the stock scripts delete saves before writing their
  replacement and `File.close` cannot report failure.

Done when: a scripted career session survives save, quit and reload with
identical car state and money.

## M6 — Robustness and platform

Goal: malformed data and long sessions cannot crash or damage the
installation. Can run alongside the others.

- Open review risks: DirectShow frame copy bounds, TUFA and RPK count
  checks, file-root confinement and `File.delete`, TREE integer semantics and
  the missing remainder operator, the duplicate Splash enter, retained OSD
  widgets, XInput fallback.
- Display-mode enumeration and controller options documented and validated.
- Optional: crash logging, native call reports and physics telemetry, all
  designed on the PR #3 branch.

Done when: each listed risk is fixed or explicitly accepted, with a note in
the knowledge base.
